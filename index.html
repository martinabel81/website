Martin Abel

Associate Professor of Economics, Bowdoin College

I am a development, labor and behavioral economist. Much of my work uses field and online experiments to study discrimination and gender in labor markets, how people search for jobs, and how behavioral tools can change what people do. A lot of it is set in South Africa, with projects in India, Mexico and Saudi Arabia.

I am a Research Fellow at IZA and an Affiliate at J-PAL. I hold a PhD and an MPA in International Development from Harvard University and a Diplom from the University of Jena.
